# Pricing Classification Standards

PathFinder enforces an unambiguous, zero-deception pricing taxonomy for all learning resources.

## 1. Classification Categories
1. `GENUINELY_FREE`: Lessons, exercises, and all curriculum materials are completely accessible without payment or subscription (e.g. MIT OpenCourseWare, freeCodeCamp, Microsoft Learn).
2. `FREE_TO_ENROLL_PAID_CERTIFICATE`: Learning content is freely accessible (audit mode), but an optional paid certificate or exam fee applies (e.g. NPTEL ₹1000 exam fee, Coursera audit).
3. `YOUTUBE_FREE_CONTENT`: High quality, verified educational playlists hosted openly on YouTube.
4. `SUBSCRIPTION_REQUIRED`: Access requires an ongoing recurring subscription (e.g. Coursera Plus, LinkedIn Learning).
5. `PAID`: Upfront purchase required to access curriculum.
6. `UNKNOWN`: Pricing terms could not be authoritatively verified.

## 2. Integrity Rules
- Free trial $\neq$ Genuinely Free.
- Free enrollment $\neq$ Free certificate.
- Subscription $\neq$ Free.
- Unknown $\neq$ Free.
- Never retain a stale "Free" badge after external data indicates pricing has changed.
